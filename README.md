# Álex Jiménez Ruiz

Industrial engineer (BSc and MSc, University of Girona) working at the intersection of **simulation, software and mechanical design**. I like taking products from the model to the real world: digital twins, finite-element simulators, machine design and hands-on prototyping.

👉 **Portfolio:** [alexitojr9.github.io](https://alexitojr9.github.io)

## What I work on

- **Simulation and digital twins**: physical modelling, fault detection and diagnosis (FDD), predictive maintenance
- **Machine design**: CAD, FEM validation, design for manufacturing
- **R&D**: turning research ideas into working tools and prototypes

**Main tools:** MATLAB · Simulink · Python · Ansys · SolidWorks · Arduino
